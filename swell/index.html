Surf report

Conditions at the end of the month.

The plates

Charts for people who check the tide twice.

Plate I

Where they sit now

Momentum

Up and to the right is the big one. Follows the day set in the time machine.

How it is read

Across is everything posted so far. Up is how the last three days compare with the three before. Bubble size is the number of voices.

Plate II

The forecaster’s report card

Backtest

Every morning it had to guess the next three days using only the past. Here is how that went.

How it is read

Damped Holt smoothing is compared with simply assuming the next three days repeat the last three. Lower error is better. An alarm counts as right when the trend gets at least 5 more posts in the next three days.

Plate III

The tide table

Trend by day

Thirty days of water. Each bright patch is a set rolling through.

How it is read

Brighter means more posts carrying that hashtag on that day. Trends are ordered by total size.

Plate IV

Anatomy of a wave

Lifecycle

How long each one took to rise, how hard it broke, and how long the foam hung about.

How it is read

Rise is the days from first post to the busiest day. Crest is posts on that day. Afterlife is the days after the crest that still saw at least one post in five of the crest’s size.

Plate V

First to hear

Platform order

Each dot is a platform catching the wave. The further right, the later to the party.

How it is read

Days between a trend’s first post anywhere and its first post on each platform.

Plate VI

Words on the rise

Keywords

Never mind the hashtags. These are the plain words creeping into the water.

How it is read

How often each English word appeared in the second half of the month compared with the first, per thousand posts. Short and common words are left out.

Plate VII

How it gets carried

Reposts vs talk

Some waves are pushed by conversation. Others just get passed along.

How it is read

Each trend’s posts split by kind: original, reply, quote, repost and share across platforms.

How the forecast is cast

Four steps, and one rule it may never break.

1

Spot

Hashtags shared by at least 5 voices earn a place on the chart.

2

Measure

Three-day momentum is scored against the trend’s own quiet baseline.

3

Project

Damped Holt smoothing leans on the recent past and stops itself getting carried away.

4

Own up

Every guess is scored against what really happened, and the score stays on the page.

House rulesPeeking at tomorrowDeleting bad guessesCalling a ripple a tsunami
Launches come from nowhereNo model can see a hashtag that does not exist yet.
Waves are short hereWith only a few active days each, forecasts have little to learn from.
One month, one seaA good score on this sample promises nothing about the next one.
