How it is read

The needle follows a small model trained on 1.6 million tweets, running on your device. The coloured words are the ones that pushed it hardest. The word-list score beside it is the simpler method from the paper. Both are English only, and both can miss a joke.

Plate VIII

Classifier method

Paper §6.2.2

A clerk who has only ever read the labelled posts, and now has opinions about yours.

How it is read

A multinomial Naive Bayes over a bag of words, trained in your browser. It sits two exams. The first holds back a fifth of the posts. The second holds back a fifth of the distinct wordings, so every question is new. The demo flock is written from only a few dozen phrases, so the clerk can memorise them but learns little it can carry to a fresh sentence, and the second score shows it. Real files tend to bring the two scores closer.

Plate IX

Sentiment confidence

Paper §6.2.1

First, is it an opinion at all? Then, how firmly was it meant? Click a band to read it.

warmevensour
How it is read

The word list scores opinion words +1 or -1, flips them after a negation and boosts them after "very" or an exclamation mark. No opinion words means a plain statement. Only posts in English or with no language tag are read.

Plate X

Model evaluation

Measured

Marked against text that people labelled by hand, which the model never saw while it learned.

How it is read

The reader is a logistic regression over words and word pairs, with negation marked. It learned from Sentiment140, 1.6 million tweets labelled by their emoticons. The exams are Sentiment140’s hand-labelled tweets and UCI’s Sentiment Labelled Sentences. Neither was used for training or for choosing settings. Marks fall on sarcasm, slang and language that differs from what it studied, and it will not judge posts that are not in English.

Sentiment methodology

Four instruments, and a few things no forecaster should promise.

1

Read

Each post arrives with a mood: warm, even or sour.

2

Sort

Wording rules name the feeling, from excitement to sarcasm.

3

Average

Days, trends and groups are rolled up. Warm minus sour gives the temperature.

4

Announce

The numbers become weather, because nobody ever argued with a cloud.
